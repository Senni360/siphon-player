Home finally shows your music, beats get their own place, your phone and computer keep up with each other, and play and pause answer at once.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself. If you're new, or on an earlier preview, download it from https://siphon-friends.vercel.app, extract the whole zip, and run `siphon.exe`.

## New

**Home**

- **Beats for you.** Type beats and instrumentals have a shelf of their own, so the big card and New releases are songs.
- **One New releases.** New albums, EPs and singles from the artists you play, from Spotify and SoundCloud together, in one shelf instead of one per source.
- The songs beside the big card show when they're playing, and pause under the pointer.

**Your phone and your computer**

- **Playing on your phone.** When your phone plays music itself, your computer shows it over the player, with play and pause for it and **Play here**, which carries the song on here from the same place and pauses the phone.
- **At once.** With Encore open on your phone, what you press there reaches your computer in about half a second, and what changes on your computer shows on the phone just as quickly.

## Better

- **Play and pause answer at once.** The button shows what you pressed straight away, without a spinner, and a quick cross-fade replaces the ring that rippled out of it.

## Fixed

- Home showed only popular YouTube beats for listeners with plenty of history. It asked for recommendations before Spotify and your likes had arrived and then waited half an hour. It now asks again as soon as they're there.
- Someone who just doesn't use YouTube is no longer treated as new to music.
- Encore on your phone no longer signs you out after a hiccup. It stays signed in as long as you open it at least every 90 days. That was fixed for every version already.

## Good to know

- "Playing on your phone" and the faster phone link need this version on your computer. Your phone gets its half by reloading the page.
