Home is rebuilt around what you listen to, you can connect a YouTube account, and pausing then skipping no longer plays a bit of the old song.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself. If you're new, or on an earlier preview, download it from https://siphon-friends.vercel.app, extract the whole zip, and run `siphon.exe`.

## New

**Home**

- **Rebuilt, with SoundCloud and YouTube in it.** Recommendations from all your sources sit together, tuned to what you play and like.
- **No sideways scrolling.** Home shows what fits. Elsewhere, rows that run long have clear arrows, and the sideways scrollbars are gone.
- **No podcasts or audiobooks on Home.** They're still in your library and Search. In Search, choose their chip or scroll to the bottom of the results.

**YouTube account**

- **Connect YouTube if you want to.** Your subscriptions and likes then sync with your real account. Browsing and playing still need no account.
- It takes your own free Google Cloud project, and the dialog walks you through it. Your keys stay on your computer and nothing goes through a server. Google warns that the app is unverified, as it is.

**Group**

- **Short audio clears once you've seen it.** It comes back marked when there's a new comment or a new one arrives.

## Fixed

- **No more of the old song after a skip.** Pausing, skipping and then pressing play used to sound about 50 ms of the previous song first. This was true for every source. Pausing and skipping now take a moment longer behind the scenes, and the longer your audio buffer setting, the longer that is.
- **Follow on SoundCloud** now uses the request SoundCloud's own site does. If SoundCloud refuses an unfollow, the button goes back.
- **Follow on YouTube** no longer disappears if you press it just after opening the app.
- **The player's outline** now lights around the whole bar, not only near the middle.

## Good to know

- Google sign-in, SoundCloud Follow and the personal recommendations haven't been tried on a real account yet. Tell me if any of them misbehave.
- The audio fix hasn't been listened to yet either, so tell me if you still hear the old song.
