Siphon 0.15.0-pre.5 rebuilds the group: your Spotify account is your place in it, whoever makes a group runs it, and listening parties are now something you can see and can't fall out of by accident.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, Siphon offers this update by itself. From an earlier preview, extract the whole zip into a writable folder and open `siphon.exe`. The executable is unsigned, so Windows may ask before running it.

## New

- **Your groups follow your Spotify account.** Press Connect Spotify on the Group page once on each computer and your groups, name and shares come along. You keep everything you've already shared, and two identities of yours become one. Link SoundCloud too, or unlink either, from the People tab.
- **Groups you can run.** Create a group in the app and you own it: add and remove people, make a new invite code, make admins, and choose who may share, host, add to a queue or invite. You can also see and sign out your other computers.
- **Parties with a host.** Listen along and channels are now one idea. A strip on every page says you're in a party, whose it is and how to leave.
- **No more falling out, or skipping for everyone.** Guests can't seek, pause or skip until they press Leave. Next asks the host to skip, and the host can share the controls or hand the party over.
- **Add songs the normal way.** In a party, search as usual and any song you press joins the queue instead of playing. Pasting a link still works.
- **Background playlists.** Pick a whole playlist to play when the party's queue runs out.
- **Share playlists.** Send a playlist to the group as a copy, for anyone to save, play or use in a party.
- **A Group page you can find things on.** It's now split into Now, Songs, Playlists, Stats and People. Songs can be searched by song, artist or person, and conversations stay folded until you open them.

## Good to know

Connect Spotify sends your app's Spotify access token to the group service once, to prove who you are. The service reads your account id with it and doesn't keep it, but until it expires, within the hour, that token could act on your Spotify account, so you are trusting whoever runs the service for that long. Nothing is sent until you press the button.

Connect Spotify has not yet been tried with a real Spotify account, and parties have been tested against the group service, but not yet between two people playing music. Please report anything odd. Everyone in a party needs this version: on an older one, a guest's controls are refused rather than locked.

Discord presence is included but stays off until its Discord application is set up. Cross-platform release checks are not complete; this preview was built and checked on Windows.
