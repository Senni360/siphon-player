Your phone finds a copy of every Spotify song that plays, and keeps your playlists ready while this computer is off.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself.

## Fixed

- **Spotify songs on your phone find a copy that actually plays.** Many were matched to a SoundCloud copy SoundCloud only serves encrypted, which never played. Encore now leaves those out and finds the song's official YouTube upload instead, looking at the album on YouTube Music, a YouTube search and SoundCloud at once. Every Spotify song in the 2626 playlist now plays on the phone.
- **A YouTube song plays on your phone away from home.** Its address only worked on this computer's own connection, so it failed on mobile data. This computer now puts the song on your server, and the phone plays it from there.
- **A wrong match is found again.** An instrumental could be saved as the song, and an artist's own upload could be passed over. Matches saved before this version are checked once more.
- **A new album that would not play now says why.** Spotify plays many new releases only in its own player. Encore skips such a song as before, but no longer restarts your Spotify session over it, and tells you once that **Soloist**, Spotify's own player, plays them here.

## New

- **Your playlists are made ready on your server ahead of time.** While this computer is on, it works through the Spotify songs in your mixed playlists, a few seconds per song, so the phone plays them with this computer off.
- **Albums look like albums on Home.** The large card shows a new album with its record behind the cover and what it is, "Album · 12 songs". A song pick stays a plain cover.
- **BPM and key are measured from the song itself.** The BPM/key button on a row now works: it listens to the song and shows its tempo and key to copy.
- **Encore carries on when another app takes your speakers.** When FL Studio or another program holds the audio device, Encore waits and picks up the song where it stopped once the device is free.
- **Download on your computer, from your phone.** A song's menu on the phone sends it here to download.

## Better

- **Your phone browses with this computer off.** Home, your library, playlists, liked songs, albums, artists and search come from your server, and the phone no longer says this computer is off while that works.
- **The phone shows new releases**, with albums distinct from singles.
- **The phone says which songs need this computer.** With it off, a playlist marks the songs your server has no copy of yet instead of skipping them.
- **Lyrics on the phone** now show the words of the song the phone is playing.
- **Find in a long playlist** on the phone, and a **sleep timer** in the playing song's menu.
