## Fixed

- **The window no longer freezes while the disk is busy.** 0.13.0 saved settings, the session and history on the window's own thread, so under heavy disk use the window could stop responding and Pause or a click could wait for seconds. They are now saved in the background, and anything waiting is still written before Spotifast quits or the computer sleeps. If the window ever stalls again, the log now says so, which helps when you report it. By @crmne.
