Pre-save upcoming albums, and add songs to your playlists from your phone.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself.

## New

- **Pre-save from an artist's page.** When an artist has an album coming out, their page shows it above Popular, with when it comes out and a **Pre-save** button. The card opens its countdown page.
- **Pre-save from your phone.** The artist's page on your phone shows the same, and pre-saves through this computer.
- **Add to playlist on your phone.** A song's menu offers your playlists, the ones you used last first, mixed playlists included.

## Fixed

- **A liked song's menu on your phone said Like.** Pressing it took the like away while saying "Liked". It now offers **Remove from your likes** for a song that is liked.
