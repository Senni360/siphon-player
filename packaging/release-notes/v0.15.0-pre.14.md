Artists now have one page across Spotify, SoundCloud and YouTube, Encore works from your phone, and new YouTube songs start faster.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself. If you're new, or on an earlier preview, download it from https://siphon-friends.vercel.app, extract the whole zip, and run `siphon.exe`.

## New

**One artist page**

Open an artist on Spotify and the page also shows what they have elsewhere. It finds their SoundCloud and YouTube accounts by itself. If it isn't sure which account is theirs, it asks, and it remembers your answer.

- **Not on Spotify.** Their SoundCloud and YouTube songs that Spotify doesn't have, in one list.
- **Also posts as.** Another account of theirs, when their own bio names it.
- **Produced by.** Songs by others that credit them as producer in the title.
- **Snippets and unreleased.** Snippets, leaks and scrapped songs, with links to the accounts posting them.
- **Edits and remixes.** Other people's versions of their songs.
- **Play everything** queues their top songs and the songs that aren't on Spotify together.
- A SoundCloud or YouTube profile links back to the full artist page, when the artist is known.

**Encore on your phone**

- Open https://encore.senni.dev on your phone. Ask me for the code to get in.
- In Encore on your computer, go to Settings, then Your phone, and press **Link a phone**. Type the code it shows into your phone.
- Your phone then has the same account and groups. It can browse your library, search all three sources, open artist pages, and play on your computer.
- You can also sign in to Spotify on your phone, so your Spotify library still works with the computer off. This needs `https://encore.senni.dev/callback` added to your Spotify app.

A phone browser can't play Spotify, so the sound comes from your computer or your other Spotify devices.

**Undo**

Removing songs from a playlist or anything from your library now offers Undo. Songs go back exactly where they were.

## Faster

- **New YouTube songs start in about half the time.** Rest the pointer on a song for a moment first and it should start almost at once.

## Fixed

- **Search fields** no longer show a second border inside the first.
- **Full-screen lyrics** are easier to read on pale covers, and scale up on large screens.
- **The player** no longer cuts across the library on wider windows.
- **Home** no longer keeps an empty card at the top when there's nothing to show.

## Good to know

- Artist pages and the phone app haven't been tried on real accounts yet. "Not on Spotify" may list a song Spotify does have, so tell me if you see one.
- An artist's first visit takes several seconds to fill in. After that it shows at once.
- The first time you open the phone app, it can take up to 15 seconds to reach your computer.
- The Google setup steps for connecting YouTube changed: it now asks for a home page and privacy address, which the dialog gives you.
