Beats show their own names everywhere, the player bar tells you a beat's tempo and key, and YouTube songs start sooner. And if Spotify ever refused a play command with a bare error, Encore now tries again the way the Spotify app does.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself. If you're new, or on an earlier preview, download it from https://siphon-friends.vercel.app, extract the whole zip, and run `siphon.exe`.

## New

**Beats**

- **The beat's own name.** A beat's upload title packs several ideas into one line: [FREE] tags, who it's "for", a style, a tempo. Where a track is a beat, Encore now shows the beat's own name everywhere it appears - the quoted name when the producer wrote one, like "tic tac" or "Tweaker" - on Home, on cards, in rows, in the queue and by the player.
- Under the name it says who the beat is for ("for Young Nudy x Lucki") or its style ("UK rap") when there are no artists, and the producer stays where the uploader is usually shown.
- **Tempo and key.** A beat's BPM and key show beside the artists in the player bar, like "140 BPM - C sharp minor". When the producer stated them in the title or the description YouTube and SoundCloud hand over, those show at once.
- **Heard for itself.** When a beat says nothing about its tempo, Encore listens and works it out itself: after ten seconds of playing (a setting in Settings, from 0 to 60 seconds, or Never), the tempo and key it heard fade into the player bar. The analysis runs once per song and nothing is written or sent anywhere. A beat's stated tempo always wins over the one heard.

**YouTube**

- **Songs start sooner.** A pressed YouTube song no longer queues behind searches and page listings, so it starts as soon as before, even while you browse hard. While a song plays, the link of the queued YouTube song after the next one is found ahead of time, so a skip stays instant.

## Fixed

- Spotify sometimes refused a play, pause or queue command for Soloist or some speakers with a bare "Restriction violated" and nothing else. Encore now sends such a command again over the Connect state service, the way Spotify's own apps do; and when there's no way to reach the player, it says which setting would help instead of Spotify's bare refusal.

## Good to know

- The tempo-and-key setting is new in Settings. It listens only to beats and instrumentals, never to Spotify's own decoded audio, and takes nothing from songs with a stated tempo.
