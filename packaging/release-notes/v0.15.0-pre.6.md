Siphon is now **Siphon: Encore**, shown as Encore, with a new logo. Discord presence is switched on too, and it shows things no other player's card can.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself. From an earlier preview, extract the whole zip into a writable folder and open `siphon.exe`. The executable is unsigned, so Windows may ask before running it.

[Download the Windows x64 ZIP](https://github.com/Senni360/siphon-player/releases/download/v0.15.0-pre.6/siphon-v0.15.0-pre.6-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc.zip) · [SHA-256 checksums](https://github.com/Senni360/siphon-player/releases/download/v0.15.0-pre.6/checksums.txt) · [signature](https://github.com/Senni360/siphon-player/releases/download/v0.15.0-pre.6/checksums.txt.sig)

## New

- **A new name and logo.** The app reads Encore everywhere you see it, and the full name appears in the places that say exactly what this is, like About and the sign-in footer. Your sign-in, library and start-at-login all carry over.
- **Encore counts your encores.** Keep coming back to a song and your Discord card says so: "Encore ×3 · 4 plays today".
- **The sound of the song, on your card.** A few bars of what's actually playing, redrawn every 15 seconds or so. They only appear for music this computer is playing.
- **Your group, without names.** The card can say that others in your group are on the same song right now, or that a song was shared in your group. It only ever shows a count, never who.
- **A livelier card.** Your status in the member list names the song instead of the app, the title and artist link to it, and the logo pulses in the corner of the artwork while music plays.

Discord presence is on by default while Discord is open, and you can turn it off in the Group page's People tab. A song from a private SoundCloud link only ever shows "Listening on Encore".

## Good to know

Discord accepted this card in testing, but how it looks on a profile, including whether the badge animates and how the bars draw, has not been checked by eye yet. Please report anything odd.

From pre.5, these are still true: Connect Spotify has not yet been tried with a real Spotify account, and parties have not yet been tested between two people playing music. Windows x64 is now the only platform built and checked. The Windows files keep the name `siphon` for now.
