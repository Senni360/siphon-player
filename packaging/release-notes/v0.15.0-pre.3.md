Siphon 0.15.0-pre.3 brings the group in: a Group page for your friends' shares and threads, and a group service that quietly learns what each of you likes. It also makes this the last preview you install by hand: from here on, Siphon updates itself.

This is a Windows x64 preview. The portable executable is unsigned, so Windows may ask before running it. **Install this one by hand**: extract the whole zip into a writable folder and open `siphon.exe`. Earlier previews cannot verify Siphon's new release signature, so they will not offer this update themselves; this one will offer the next.

## New

- **Siphon updates itself.** It checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for updates**, offers a preview the next preview, and installs only releases signed with Siphon's own release key.
- **A Group page for the people you share with.** Threads, direct shares to some of the group, and dragging a shared song straight into your DAW as a file. (#8)
- **The group is live.** Siphon now connects to its own group service. Ask the owner for the invite code, then join from the Group page.
- **Your group feed learns your taste.** Friends' shares you will probably love lead Home's **From the group**, and a share you probably won't care about no longer raises the unread badge. A share sent to you always does.
- **Autoplay flows into your friends' music.** When a list ends, Siphon carries on with the friend's Spotify share that best continues what you were playing, when one clearly fits, and Spotify's autoplay continues from there.
- **Cleaner SoundCloud and YouTube titles.** Uploads named like "Artist - Song [FREE DL]" or "Song (Official Video)" show as the song and its artist once read, and keep that name.
- **A new look and motion throughout.** The page sits on a glass card over the playing song's glow, with motion for entrances, rows, hearts, dialogs, Now playing and lyrics. (#8)

The group's suggestions come from TypeSafe AI's Jev decision model, run by the group service on the owner's key. Only song titles, artist and uploader names, and your own shares and hearts are sent, and only while you are in a group. A slow or failed answer changes nothing.

Cross-platform release checks are not complete; this preview was built and checked on Windows.
