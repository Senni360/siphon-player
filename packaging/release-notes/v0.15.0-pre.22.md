Credits work again, your stats are yours, and a batch of fixes.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself.
## New

- **Show credits** in a song's right-click menu.
- **Stats count only what you play in Encore.** Songs from the Spotify app are left out. To count them again, turn on **Count plays from the Spotify app** on the Stats page. History you imported before this update still counts; clear your stats once if you don't want it.

## Fixed

- **Spotify credits never loaded.** Spotify had changed how its credits are asked for.
- **The Lyrics tab did nothing.** It now shows the song's lyrics, or says there are none.
- **The playing song could show twice in the queue.**
- **A dropped connection lost play, pause and seek.** They're now tried again before giving up.
- **One unreadable setting reset all your settings.** Now only that setting goes back to its default, and the old file is kept as `settings.json.unreadable`.
- **A stalled update download stuck on Downloading** until Encore restarted.
- **Group errors blamed Spotify** when the group service was the one not answering.

## Better

- **Encore stops drawing while it's hidden or minimised**, and draws less while it isn't focused, so it uses less of your computer in the background.
- **The phone plays songs from the always-on server sooner**, starting before the whole song has arrived.
