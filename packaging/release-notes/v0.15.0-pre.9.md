Share your own songs with the group, and a cleaner, roomier Encore.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself. If you're new, or on an earlier preview, extract the whole zip and open `siphon.exe`. The executable is unsigned, so Windows may ask before running it.

## New

- **Shared files.** Drop audio files anywhere in Encore, or right-click a local song, and everyone in your group can play them. They're in Group under Shared files. Up to 15 minutes and 40 MB a song, in MP3, FLAC, M4A or AAC. When the shared space fills up, the oldest songs are deleted.
- **Share what's playing in one press.** Use the new button on the player, or press `G`.
- **Group in search.** What your friends shared comes first, and new files from friends show on Home.
- **Party invites you can click.** Copy invite gives a web link that opens the party in Encore. There's also a Leave button on the player.
- **Share a playlist.** Right-click a playlist and choose Share with friends.
- **Streamer mode.** Find it in Settings. It hides your Client ID, account names, invite codes, device and computer names, and file paths. Friends' names still show.

## Better

- The panels now run to the bottom of the window, with the player floating over them.
- The Group page has simpler tabs, and the group switcher is a menu by the title.
- Text fields and switches are easier to see. Settings has shortcuts to each section.
- Soloist reconnects if its connection drops, restarts if it stops, and comes back by itself after a mixed playlist. This is still being tested, so tell us if it drops.

## Good to know

Spotify may refuse librespot's audio key for some people, which stops Spotify songs on the built-in player. Soloist is the way around that, and it needs your own Soloist key from Spotify. SoundCloud, YouTube and shared files still play.
