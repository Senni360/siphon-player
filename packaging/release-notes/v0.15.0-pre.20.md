A big one. The player's controls move properly now, every song you hear in Encore counts in your stats, downloads are much faster, and Settings finally makes sense.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself.

## New

- **Download settings.** Choose your download folder, format and quality in Settings, under Downloads & files.
- **The drag tray.** A finished download pops up in the Siphon tray, ready to drag into FL Studio. You can turn it off in Settings.
- **Up to four downloads at once.**
- **More than one phone.** Link a second phone, or another browser on the same one, from Settings, under Devices.

## Better

- **Spotify downloads are much quicker.** Finding the song now takes one search instead of six, and a song found once is remembered, for downloads and for your phone.
- **Local files start sooner.** Rows work right away instead of waiting for your folder to be read, the folder is remembered between launches, and a mixed playlist starts from what's already loaded.
- **The controls.** The play button stays where it is and Play and Pause turn into each other. Repeat goes around, Shuffle flips, and a heart fills and throws off sparks.
- **Home.** The featured card no longer clashes with the colour of what's playing and has lost its cone of light. Tiles, tabs and the search bar let the page show through. Albums and EPs look different from singles in New releases.
- **Settings is reorganised** into Account, Devices, Playback, Equalizer, Downloads & files, Appearance, Privacy, Network, Updates and About. SoundCloud and YouTube connect from Account.
- **YouTube recommendations are beats only.** No more concerts, music videos or regular songs.

## Fixed

- **Your stats were missing almost everything you played in Encore.** Each song was thrown away as the next one began. From now on, Spotify, SoundCloud and YouTube plays all count. Plays from before this update can't be recovered.
- **Leaving a group deleted your whole account.** The old button took your other groups and linked phones with it, and left no way back. It now only leaves that group, and your group's invite code brings you back.

## Good to know

- The Podcasts tab is gone from the library.
- Encore's phone app was updated separately and has no site code any more. Open `encore.senni.dev` and link it with a code from Settings.
