Siphon 0.15.0-pre.1 is the first preview of Siphon: the player now has a new look, plays SoundCloud alongside Spotify in one queue, and mixes both in your own playlists. This is a Windows-only preview, built and checked by hand rather than by the usual release pipeline, and it has not yet been tried on a real Windows install. Expect rough edges.

**Download Siphon:** [Windows](https://github.com/Senni360/siphon-player/releases/download/v0.15.0-pre.1/siphon-v0.15.0-pre.1-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc.zip)

Extract the whole zip into a writable folder and open `siphon.exe`. The executable is not signed, so Windows may ask before running it. There is no Mac, Linux, Flatpak or installer build for this preview.

## New

- **Siphon has a new name, icon and look.** The player floats as an island at the bottom of the window, and a new song swipes the old one off. The sidebar floats as a card and slides in from the edge when hidden. Your existing profile moves over safely.
- **Now playing grows out of the player island,** and its Up next follows the queue as it changes.
- **Every song row has its four tools at hand,** and the album column narrows before it gives way, so titles keep their room in small windows.
- **Each title shows where it plays from by its colour:** green for Spotify and orange for SoundCloud, in both light and dark themes.
- **Small touches tell you what happened:** a heart pops when you like a song, a band of light crosses the row that starts playing, a song that can't play shakes its title, and new messages rise into place above the island.
- **SoundCloud plays in the same queue as Spotify.** Search shows SoundCloud results alongside Spotify's, previews are labelled before they play, and you can connect a SoundCloud account to reach your own likes and sets.
- **Mixed playlists** hold songs from both sources, with the same editing as Spotify playlists. You can also add SoundCloud songs and sets to a Spotify playlist on this computer.
- **Downloads through the installed Siphon helper,** from a row or from Now playing.

## Fixed

- **Liked Songs plays from the whole list,** not only the songs loaded so far. (#1085)
- **Local songs with special characters in their names play.** (#1091)
- **The queue survives a refused audio key** and Spotify Connect hiccups, instead of stopping or pausing on its own.
- **Signing in as someone else never shows the last account's playlist or songs.**
