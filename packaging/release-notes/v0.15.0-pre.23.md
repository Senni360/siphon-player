Your phone works with this computer off, and more Spotify songs find a copy that plays.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself.

## New

- **Your phone keeps going while this computer is off.** Encore keeps your playlists, queue and
  saved matches on your server, and pause, play and seek reach the phone at once. The switch is
  in **Settings > Devices > Your phone**: **Use my server while this computer is off**.
- **Your own files on your phone.** With **Play my local files on my phone** on, the songs in your
  playlists are stored on your server, so the phone plays them without this computer being on.
- **Your phone can use your sign-ins from the server.** They are stored encrypted there, so search
  and home still work while this computer is off. Off by default for everyone but you.

## Fixed

- **More Spotify songs find the copy that plays.** Matching now folds accents and spelling
  differences, and keeps version words like "remix" or "sped up" out of the title it compares, so
  a song with a SoundCloud copy plays instead of going quiet.
- **A saved SoundCloud match could not be played by the phone.** It is now sent in the form the
  server understands.
- **The phone could retry a refused sync forever**, and saving the queue also rewrote the
  playlists.
- **Song titles in another script were compared with their marks dropped**, so two different
  words could count as the same song.

## Better

- **The phone has new screens.** One player that opens into a full Now Playing sheet, with the
  queue, the lyrics and where it plays; search that answers as you type; a queue you can reorder,
  swipe away and undo; and settings for where the music plays.
