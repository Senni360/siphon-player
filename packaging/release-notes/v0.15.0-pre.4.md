Siphon 0.15.0-pre.4 makes the group something you do together: see what your friends are playing, join them, share a queue, and see your listening stats.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3, Siphon offers this update by itself. From an earlier preview, extract the whole zip into a writable folder and open `siphon.exe`. The executable is unsigned, so Windows may ask before running it.

## New

- **See what friends are playing.** The Group page lists each friend's song once they agree to share. Siphon asks you once, and you can change your answer in your group settings.
- **Play song or Join.** Play song plays it just for you. Join listens along with them, and they lead.
- **Channels.** Start a channel and everyone in it can add songs, reorder them, skip and pause, with Spotify, SoundCloud and YouTube in one queue.
- **Listening stats.** The Stats tab shows minutes by month, top songs, artists, albums and genres, streaks and when you listen, and compares you with friends who share. Import your Extended Streaming History from Spotify for every play since your account began.
- **A tidier sidebar.** SoundCloud is now one dropdown that starts collapsed, so followed artists no longer fill the sidebar.

Everyone plays on their own account. The group only receives your current song, your totals and the top of each list, and only if you share. Listening together has been tested against the live group service, but not yet between two people playing music.

Cross-platform release checks are not complete; this preview was built and checked on Windows.
