A small fix for pre.20.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself.

## Fixed

- **Starting a mixed playlist before it had fully loaded could lose your saved queue.** A playlist with Spotify songs and local files now waits until it's loaded before it starts.
- **Restarting a song counted it twice in your stats.**
- **Local files in a playlist could show as unplayable** while your folder was still being read.
