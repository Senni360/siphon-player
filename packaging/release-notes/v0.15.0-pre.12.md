You can now browse YouTube and SoundCloud properly inside Encore, the player floats over the page on frosted glass, and there's a lot more motion.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself. If you're new, or on an earlier preview, download it from https://siphon-friends.vercel.app, extract the whole zip, and run `siphon.exe`.

## New

**YouTube**

- **Search YouTube.** There's a YouTube chip in Search, and YouTube shows under the other sources in All. It finds videos, channels and playlists.
- **Channel pages.** Click a channel's name to see its banner, subscribers, videos as thumbnails, and playlists.
- **Follow channels.** New uploads in the sidebar shows what they last posted. Follows stay on your computer, and no Google account is needed.
- **Start a mix** from any video by right-clicking it.
- Pasting a channel or playlist link opens its page.

YouTube needs the Siphon helper installed, as playing YouTube links already did.

**SoundCloud**

- **Discover.** Genres, SoundCloud's own selections, and what's trending. No account needed.
- **Profiles.** A banner, Follow, and the tabs Tracks, Popular, Albums, Playlists, Reposts, Likes and Following.
- **Related tracks** from any song by right-clicking it.
- **Your feed** shows what the people you follow posted, once SoundCloud is connected.
- Rows show play counts.

**Look and motion**

- **The player floats over the page.** Songs scroll behind it on frosted glass, and its words stay readable over any cover. Now Playing runs behind it too.
- **Many new animations**, across hovering, lists, cards, page changes, the player, Now Playing and lyrics. Windows' reduced motion setting still turns them off.
- The custom title bar is on by default for new installs. If you've already chosen, your choice stays.

## Fixed

- **Songs animate in all the way down the screen.** They used to stop at the tenth row on a large or zoomed-out screen.
- The library row for the page you're on no longer disappears into the song's glow.

## Good to know

- Following someone on SoundCloud is new and hasn't been tried on a real account yet. If SoundCloud refuses, the button goes back and tells you.
- Some SoundCloud songs only play as previews without a Go+ subscription, as on SoundCloud itself.
- YouTube's feed doesn't give a length for new uploads, so New uploads shows them without one.
