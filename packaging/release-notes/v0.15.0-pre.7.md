Music links you click in Discord can now open straight in Encore, and the Discord card's badge takes on the colour of the song you're playing.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself. From an earlier preview, extract the whole zip into a writable folder and open `siphon.exe`. The executable is unsigned, so Windows may ask before running it.

## New

- **Links open in Encore.** Spotify, SoundCloud and YouTube Music links you click in Discord or any other app open in Encore, and every other link goes on to your browser. To turn it on, open a terminal in Encore's folder and run `.\siphon.exe --set-up-web-links`, then choose Encore for HTTP and HTTPS on the Windows page that opens.
- **Beats on normal YouTube open in Encore too.** Encore judges by the video's title and channel, such as "type beat" or "prod.", so now and then a beat will stay in the browser or another video will open in Encore.
- **A badge in your song's colour.** While music plays, the logo in the corner of your Discord card lights up in the colour the cover gives the window's glow.

## Fixed

- **`spotify:` links stop opening old copies.** They now reach Encore when they pointed at nothing, at a program that's gone, or at an older copy of this player. Spotify's own app keeps its links.

## Changed

- **The sound bars are gone from the Discord card.** Discord updates a card too rarely for them to look like anything.

## Good to know

Keep Encore's folder somewhere permanent before turning links on, not in Downloads. If that folder is deleted, clicked links stop working until you start another copy of Encore or choose your browser again in Windows.

The link handling is new and hasn't been tried with real clicks from Discord yet, so please report anything odd.
