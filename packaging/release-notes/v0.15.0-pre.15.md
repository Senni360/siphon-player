Artist pages dig deeper, the phone app gets lyrics and sharing, and SoundCloud and YouTube songs find their lyrics.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself. If you're new, or on an earlier preview, download it from https://siphon-friends.vercel.app, extract the whole zip, and run `siphon.exe`.

## New

**Artist pages**

- **Sets and mixtapes.** An artist's SoundCloud sets that aren't a Spotify release.
- **Newest or most played.** "Not on Spotify" can now be sorted by date, so new uploads don't hide under old hits.
- **Followers everywhere.** The header shows followers on Spotify, SoundCloud and YouTube.

**Home**

- **New on SoundCloud from artists you follow.** Fresh uploads from the SoundCloud accounts of artists you follow on Spotify, once Encore has found those accounts by opening the artists' pages.

**On your phone**

- **Play on the phone itself.** Tap *Playing on…* and pick **This phone**. Your computer's song carries on there, and the lock screen and headphones work it. Spotify songs play as the same song from YouTube or SoundCloud, so mixed playlists run straight through.
- **Mixed playlists** in your library, and the group's **shared playlists** in the Group tab.
- **Lyrics** in Now Playing, following the song. Tap a line to jump to it.
- **Share** what's playing, or any song, with your group.
- **Jump back in** and **Made for you** on Home.
- **Accounts in search**, so you can open a SoundCloud profile or YouTube channel.
- **Follow** artists from their page.
- Going back and switching tabs keep your place, and searches are remembered.

**Settings**

- **Reduce motion**, for keeping animations to a minimum whatever Windows says.
- **Your phone** shows when your phone is connected, and lists the devices signed in as you.

## Better

- **Lyrics for SoundCloud and YouTube** are found far more often. An upload called "Artist - Song (Official Video)" is now looked up as the song.
- **Search** says All sources and All types instead of two chips called All, and the source names no longer look like headings.
- **Ctrl K** opens search, as well as Ctrl F and /.

## Fixed

- Playing a long playlist from your phone failed. That was fixed for every version already.
- Pages you opened in pre.14 now show the new artist lists straight away, without waiting a day.
- Home no longer shows a playlist right under the tile showing the same playlist.

## Good to know

- The phone needs this version on your computer for lyrics and sharing.
