Home now leads with what just came out from the artists you actually play, on any source, and YouTube on Home is music only.

This is a Windows x64 preview. On pre.3 or later, the app offers this update by itself. If you're new, or on an earlier preview, download it from https://siphon-friends.vercel.app, extract the whole zip, and run `siphon.exe`.

## New

**Home**

- **New projects first.** The big card at the top now shows the newest albums, EPs and singles from the artists you play and like most, with when they came out. The next few sit beside it, and the rest are in **New for you**. This week's releases come first, especially Friday's.
- **Works the same wherever you listen.** Encore learns your artists from what you play on Spotify, SoundCloud and YouTube, and looks for new releases where you listen to them. If you mostly use SoundCloud, your new projects come from SoundCloud.
- A release out on two sources shows once, from the one you use more.

## Better

- **YouTube on Home is music only.** Videos from channels you follow for sports, news or anything else no longer show up among your recommendations or in **New from who you follow**.
- The songs picked for you now sit under **More for you**.

## Good to know

- New Spotify releases from artists you play need local playback on, as they're read the way Spotify's own artist pages read them. Without it, new releases from the artists you follow on Spotify still show.
